# Maroon AI Suite

Build Task Tracker, a modern maroon-and-white AI administration platform for organisations managing venue rentals.

Include: AI Email Generator & Reply Assistant, Email/Meeting/Document Summariser, AI Task Planner & Scheduler, AI Research Assistant, AI Chatbot, Venue Booking & Availability, Client Management & Search, booking history, deadlines, notifications, and an admin dashboard.

Make it Outlook-inspired, responsive and easy to use. AI must support human review before important actions/emails are sent and protect client information.
